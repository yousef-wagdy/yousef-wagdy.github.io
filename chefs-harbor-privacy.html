Chef's Harbor — Privacy Policy

App: Chef's Harbor · Package: com.assc.chefsharbor · Developer: ASSC

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chef's Harbor (the "Game"), developed by ASSC ("we", "us"), handles information when you play on Android. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and the Game has no accounts, sign-in or chat.

1. Game data stored on your device

Your gameplay progress is saved locally on your device only. This includes your board, level and experience, restaurant renovations and styles, quests, purchased decorations, and your virtual balances (Cash/coins, Gems and energy). We do not upload this data to our own servers. Virtual currencies have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money.

2. Google AdMob — rewarded ads only Optional

  • The Game shows only optional, player-initiated rewarded video ads (for example "Watch a video for +15 gems"). There are no banner, interstitial (pop-up) or app-open ads.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect ads, Google may collect and process the device's Google Advertising ID (AAID), IP address (used to infer approximate location), device and app information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data.
  • Learn how Google uses this information: Google Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Google Play Billing — in-app purchases

Gem packages and the Harbor Starter Pack are sold through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed securely by Google under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. ASSC never receives, collects or stores your payment card or bank details. The Game only receives a purchase confirmation (product and order identifiers) from Google Play so it can deliver your items and restore non-consumable purchases.

4. Your consent and ad controls

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the Game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) so you can accept or refuse personalized advertising. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Opt out of personalized ads on Android: open your device Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID or turn off ad personalization.
  • You can always play the Game without watching any ads.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because game data lives on your device, you control it. To delete it, go to Android Settings → Apps → Chef's Harbor → Storage → Clear storage, use Settings → Reset progress inside the Game, or uninstall the Game. Data collected by Google for advertising or payments is retained under Google's policies linked above. Google Play purchase records are kept by Google for your account.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add new features or services. The effective date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be reflected in the Game's store listing.

8. Contact ASSC

Questions or requests about privacy in Chef's Harbor: open a request here, or use the developer contact on the Chef's Harbor Google Play page.
